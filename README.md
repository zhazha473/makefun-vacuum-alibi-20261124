## The entry

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes one line — 165 characters —
to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I checked my log first: "04:12 — spot under the sofa, 0.4 g, cleaned."
> The spot you mean never existed; your sock ate it, then you blamed the
> machine. Case closed.

## Why it fits the brief

The vacuum does not apologise — it presents evidence. A deadpan sensor log
proves the spot was cleaned, so the "missed" spot the resident complains
about never existed at all; the blame lands on the resident's sock, and the
machine closes the case. First-person, smug about its sensors, one quoted
log fragment, and the punch ("Case closed.") at the very end.

## Run

`node main.ts` — no dependencies, no network, no randomness, no clock.
Two runs write byte-identical output.
